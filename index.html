Our Story

A Homegrown Spot in Binangonan

Mambog, Binangonan, Rizal

Sikapin = Strive. Endeavor.

Agsikapin Kapé started with a simple goal: a place in Binangonan where you can get a good burger, a solid cup of coffee, and actually sit down and enjoy it.

We serve comfort food — rice meals, silog, wings, pasta, pizza, crispy pata, kare-kare, and more — paired with proper coffee and drinks. Whether you're dropping by after class, meeting friends, or grabbing a late snack, there's a seat for you.

No fuss, no pretense. Just good food in a neighborhood spot that feels like yours.
